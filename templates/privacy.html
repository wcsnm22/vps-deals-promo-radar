Third-party advertising

This site displays third-party advertising. Ads are served by third-party ad networks, not by us. Those networks may set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser in order to measure, cap or personalize advertising, and they process that data under their own privacy policies. We do not control what those networks collect and we do not receive personal data from them.

Where advertising is not currently shown on a page, this section still describes the practice for any page or future placement where it is.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links are affiliate links. If you follow one and sign up with the provider, we may earn a commission. Affiliate links may carry a referral parameter so the provider can attribute the visit. Commission never changes the prices shown on this site or which plans are listed.

What we collect ourselves

  • No accounts and no forms. There is no sign-up, no login, no comment system and no newsletter, so there is no personal information for you to submit.
  • No analytics and no tracking scripts of our own. This site does not run its own analytics and does not set first-party cookies.
  • No selling of data. We have no personal data to sell or share.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As the hosting and network provider, Cloudflare processes the request data needed to deliver and protect the site — typically IP address, user agent and the requested URL — under its own privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Outbound links

Links marked as going to a provider lead to third-party websites. Once you leave this site, those sites and their own policies apply. Please review them before buying anything.

Children
